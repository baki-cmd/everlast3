Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before upgrading your home with Everlasting uPVC windows and doors.

How long do uPVC windows last?

Our premium uPVC profiles are engineered to last 40+ years with virtually zero maintenance. They never rot, rust, warp, or fade — backed by our 20-year manufacturing warranty.

Are uPVC windows better than aluminium?

Yes — significantly. uPVC offers 5x better thermal insulation, superior sound dampening, complete corrosion resistance (no rust like aluminium), and a more refined aesthetic. They also pay back the price difference in energy savings.

How long does installation take?

Manufacturing takes 7–14 days after order confirmation. Installation is typically 1–2 days for an average home — our team works cleanly with zero damage to your walls or interiors.

Can I match windows to my interior style?

Absolutely. We offer 30+ standard colours, premium wood-grain finishes (oak, walnut, mahogany), and bespoke RAL colour matching. We also customise frame thickness, glass type, and handle design.

Do you offer warranty?

Yes — a comprehensive 20-year manufacturing warranty on our uPVC profiles. We also offer annual service contracts for absolute peace of mind.

What areas do you serve?

We deliver and install across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and Pondicherry. Pan-India shipping is available for builders and large projects.

How much do uPVC windows cost?

Pricing ranges from ₹380 to ₹1,600 per square feet based on various specifications — size, style, glass type, profile colour, and hardware. Book a free site visit and we'll share a transparent, itemised quote within 48 hours.
